This folder bundles Raid Browser v1.3.0 by Act/Horsebreed@Warmane
(https://github.com/fjaros/RaidBrowser) so Johnny's Raid Browser works without
installing it separately.

Raid Browser has no licence file. It is included here with the
author's permission, given to Johnny directly.

The files are the original ones with a single change: a two-line guard at the
top of each so they don't load when the standalone RaidBrowser addon is also
installed. _guard.lua is new. All credit for the chat parsing, raid sets and
LFR integration goes to the original author.
